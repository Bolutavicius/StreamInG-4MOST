Survey: StreamInG@4MOST

We propose 4MOST/HRS observations of stars identified as candidate
members of 9 stellar streams in the inner Milky Way, selected from
S-PLUS photometry and Gaia DR3 astrometry. This project will connect
halo substructure studies with the chemo-dynamical mapping of the disk
and bulge, offering new insights into the Galaxy’s assembly history.

2760 HR targets*

Title: Stellar Streams in the Inner Galaxy with S-PLUS and 4MOST

Brief science goal description
------------------------------
The main goals are to: (1) obtain radial velocities to constrain orbits;
(2) derive multi-element abundances to identify progenitors and disentangle
overlaps; and (3) search for chemical gradients and multiple populations in
globular cluster streams.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Source of target catalogue selection

Our data were obtained via a crossmatch between the catalog of the third
data release (DR3) of Gaia (Gaia Collaboration et al., 2023) and a catalog
of the sixth internal data release (iDR6) of S-PLUS (constructed by joining
the individual fields).

The Gaia DR3 catalog can be accessed at
    https://vizier.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/VizieR-3?-source=I/355/gaiadr3
The S-PLUS dr6 is, for now, only available internally to its collaboration
but will soon be available publicly at the splus.cloud database.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.2 Target selection overview:

The selection criteria can be summarized as follows:

[  i] An initial selection from S-PLUS iDR6 with S/N > 3 in at least one
      broad band (u, g, r, i or z) and complete photometry in all 12 filters,
      and with 12 < g < 16 mag.
[ ii] Then a cross-match with Gaia DR3 to obtain astrometry and kinematics.
      We also used the Gaia catalog to correct for dust extinction.
[iii] Application of our photometric classification method to identify stars
      likely accreted or unevolved ones (Bolutavicius et al., in prep.).
[ iv] Identification of candidate stream members with galstreams (Mateu, 2023),
      requiring χ2 consistency at the < 8 sigma level.
[  v] Final restriction to streams with at least 20 (candidate) members.

This resulted in 2670* stars to be observed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.3 Target coordinates, proper motion, and parallaxes

The proper motions and parallaxes were obtained from the Gaia DR3 catalog.
The target coordinates were obtained from S-PLUS iDR6 but converted from
EPOCH J2000 to J2027 using Astropy (SkyCoord.apply_space_motion).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.4 Target photometry

The photometry for the r band of S-PLUS iDR6 (provided in the target catalog
under MAG) was obtained using auto apertures (adaptive elliptical apertures
based on the Kron radius) for halo fields and using PSF photometry (for disk/bulge
fields). The process is very similar to what was described for the DR4 in
Herpich et al. (2024).

Although we used reddening-corrected magnitudes in the selection (e.g. when
classifying the stars into accreted and in situ), the values provided in the
target catalog are just as observed, i.e., NOT corrected for dust extinction.

1.6 Reddening

For convenience, we used the A_G values provided by the Gaia DR3 catalog
itself. We first converted those values to A_v = A_G / 0.87 and then converted
from A_v to A_{filter}. We used these last values to correct our initial
catalog for the dust extinction and perform the calculations correctly during
the selection process. But, again, the values given under the MAG column are
NOT corrected for the reddening.

The filter ratios were obtained from SVO (Rodrigo et al, 2012). For example,
the values for A_G/A_v and A_uSPLUS/A_v were obtained from, respectively:
https://svo2.cab.inta-csic.es/theory/fps/index.php?id=GAIA/GAIA3.G&&mode=browse&gname=GAIA&gname2=GAIA3#filter
https://svo2.cab.inta-csic.es/theory/fps/index.php?id=CTIO/S-PLUS.u&&mode=browse&gname=CTIO&gname2=S-PLUS#filter

The values provided in the catalog under REDDENING are simply A_v/R_v,
assuming a R_v = 3.1.
